I've wanted a place to write in longer form for a while — not papers or talks,
but the in-between thinking: why a measurement matters, what a piece of analysis
software is really doing, what I tell students who are just getting started.

## What I expect to write about

Mostly the things I work on day to day:

- **Neutrino physics** — oscillations, what NOvA and DUNE are actually measuring,
  and why hadron production (EMPHATIC) quietly underpins all of it.
- **Software & analysis** — the tooling that turns detector data into results,
  and the unglamorous engineering that makes large collaborations work.
- **Teaching & mentoring** — notes from the classroom and from advising, with an
  eye toward students who, like me, are the first in their family to take this path.

## Why here

Keeping it on my own site means it stays simple, fast, and mine — no platform in
the middle. Posts live alongside the rest of the site and share its styling.
